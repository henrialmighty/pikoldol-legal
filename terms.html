1. What Pikoldol is

Pikoldol connects pickleball players with venues. Each venue is run by its own owner, not by Pikoldol. The venue sets its courts, prices, opening hours and house rules, takes your payment and is responsible for the court you play on. Pikoldol provides the app; we don't own or operate the courts.

Pikoldol is free to use for players and court owners.

2. Booking a court (players)

  • Holds. When you pick courts, they're held for you for a short time (shown on screen) while you pay. If you don't upload your payment in time, the hold ends and the courts go back on sale.
  • Payment. You pay the venue directly with GCash, using the exact amount shown (the centavos identify your booking). Your booking is confirmed only when the venue checks your payment. The venue may reject a payment it can't find or match.
  • Honest details. Use your real name and mobile number, and upload only genuine receipts. Fake or reused receipts may get your booking rejected and reported.
  • Fair use. Don't hold courts you don't mean to pay for. The app limits how many courts can be held at once from one mobile number or connection.
  • Cancellations and refunds are set and handled by each venue, since the venue receives your money. Contact the venue (its number is on its page) about changes or refunds. If a venue can't honour a paid booking, it must refund you.
  • At the venue, follow its rules, show your booking pass when you check in, and play safely. You use the courts at your own risk, subject to the venue's own responsibility for its premises.

3. Listing a venue (court owners)

  • Eligibility. You must be at least 18 and the owner of the venue, or authorized by its owner to list and run it.
  • Accurate listing. Your location, photos, prices, hours and courts must be real and kept up to date. The GCash account must be yours or your venue's.
  • Approval. We review every venue before players can see it, and again when its GCash details change. We may ask for proof that the venue is yours, and we may decline a listing.
  • Honouring bookings. When you approve a payment, you agree to provide that court at that time. Check payments promptly and fairly, and refund players you can't serve.
