Delete your account or data

Last updated 17 November 2026

Court owners: delete your account in the app

  1. Open Pikoldol and sign in to your court owner account.
  2. If your venue is live, go to Profile and tap Delete account. If you're still setting up or waiting for approval, tap the sign-out icon at the top right, then Delete account.
  3. Check what will be deleted, enter your password, tap Confirm password, then Delete my account.

What's deleted, right away and permanently

  • Your account: name, email, mobile number and password.
  • Your venue listing: details, photos, courts, prices and GCash details.
  • All of the venue's bookings, payments, receipt screenshots, point-of-sale items and sales, and cash counts.
  • The staff accounts you added.

Before you delete: if players have paid for upcoming bookings, the app tells you how many. Contact them and refund their GCash payments first: they won't be notified.

Can't sign in?

Email loraineinthehouse@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete my Pikoldol owner account. We'll confirm it's you, delete everything listed above, and reply within 30 days.

Players: delete your booking data

Players don't have accounts. Your bookings are kept by the venue you booked as its business records, and receipt screenshots are deleted automatically 60 days after upload.

To have your name, mobile number and booking details deleted sooner, email loraineinthehouse@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Pikoldol booking data, your mobile number, and the booking code from your pass (e.g. PKD-7K3M2Q). We'll delete or anonymize them within 30 days, unless a booking is still upcoming or a payment is being disputed, in which case we'll tell you when it can be done.

Booking passes saved on your phone are removed when you uninstall the app.
