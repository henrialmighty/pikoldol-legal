Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Pikoldol is a mobile app for finding pickleball courts in the Philippines, booking them and paying the venue with GCash. Court owners use it to list their venue and run their bookings. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who sees it, how long it's kept, and the rights you have under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Who is responsible for your data: Pikoldol is run by Henri Loraine RD S. Monding, the personal information controller for the app.

Privacy questions and requests: loraineinthehouse@gmail.com. This is also how to reach our Data Protection Officer.

What we collect

When you book a court (players)

Players don't need an account. To book, you give us:

  • Your name and mobile number, so the venue knows who booked and can contact you about your booking.
  • Your booking: the venue, courts, date, times and amount.
  • A screenshot of your GCash payment and, if you enter it, the GCash reference number, so the venue can confirm you paid.

The app also handles, automatically:

  • Your location, only if you allow it, to show the nearest courts first. It is used on your phone to sort the list and is never sent to us or stored.
  • A scrambled form of your internet connection's address (IP address), kept with a booking for one day to stop people from holding courts they don't intend to pay for. We can't turn it back into your address.
  • A notification token for your phone, if you allow notifications, so we can tell you when your payment is confirmed or rejected.
  • Your booking passes (booking code, venue, times), saved only on your phone so you can find them under My bookings.

When you list a venue (court owners and their staff)

  • Your name, email address, mobile number and password. Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; nobody, including us, can read them.
  • Your venue: its name, description, address, map location, photos, courts, prices, opening hours, a contact number for players, and the GCash name and number players pay to.
  • Staff accounts you add: their name and email address.
  • What happens at your venue in the app: bookings and payments you approve or reject, court hours you block, players you check in, point-of-sale items and sales, and end-of-day cash counts.
  • Your location while you set up, only if you allow it, to place your venue's pin on the map. Only the pin you choose is saved.

Why we use it

  • To let players find venues, hold courts, book and pay, and to show venues their bookings. This is necessary to provide the service you asked for.
  • To let the venue confirm your payment and check you in.
  • To review new venues before players can see them, and to protect players from fake listings.
  • To prevent abuse, such as one person holding every court without paying.
  • To keep the app working, fix problems and keep it secure.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who sees it

  • The venue you book. Its owner and staff see your name, mobile number, booking, payment amount, receipt screenshot and GCash reference. They need these to confirm your payment and let you play.
  • Players. A venue's listing (name, address, map location, photos, prices, hours, contact number, and the GCash name and number to pay) is public in the app once approved. An owner's own name, email and mobile number are not shown to players.
  • Pikoldol, to review venues, help with problems and handle requests about your data.
  • Service providers that run parts of the app for us, under their own privacy and security commitments:
    • Supabase, which stores the app's data and files on servers in Singapore;
    • Cloudflare, which carries the app's internet traffic to those servers;
    • Expo, which delivers notifications to your phone;
    • Google, whose maps the app shows when an owner places their venue's pin.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

Because the app's data is stored in Singapore, your information is transferred outside the Philippines. We use providers that protect it with encryption in transit and access controls.

Payments happen in GCash, not in Pikoldol. You send money from your own GCash app straight to the venue; we never see or store your GCash login, PIN or balance.

How we protect it

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). The database can only be reached through the app's own server functions, which check who is asking on every request. Receipt screenshots are stored privately and shown only to the venue's staff through short-lived links. Passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach puts your data at risk we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you can:

  • Be informed about how your data is used (this policy);
  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct data that is wrong;
  • Object to its use, and withdraw consent;
  • Erase or block it (see Delete your account or data);
  • Get a copy in a usable format (data portability);
  • Complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph), and claim damages if your rights are violated.

Email loraineinthehouse@gmail.com to use any of these rights. We may ask you to confirm it's you (for players: the mobile number and booking code from your pass). We reply within 30 days.

Children

Pikoldol is meant for people 18 and over. Players under 18 should book with a parent or guardian's permission. Court owner accounts are for adults only.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For important changes, we'll also tell you in the app before they take effect.
